Field Manual · Deployment

Before the first shot

You decide where your army begins. These rules keep the battlefield readable and your army from becoming one enormous target.

1 · Choose, then place

Select a unit below your battlefield, then click a valid cell. Drag placed units to move them.

2 · Leave breathing room

Separate units may not touch, even diagonally. Leave one empty cell between different units.

3 · Larger units use linked cells

Cavalry uses two linked cells. It may be horizontal or vertical; click it to rotate.

Unit strip: The number of small squares shows how many units of that type are in play.
Gray = not placed yetGreen = placed / aliveRed = hit / destroyed
