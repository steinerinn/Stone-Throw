# Deferred cleanup and future branches

- Existing Stage 10 presentation backlog remains: projectile/animation choreography, Event Log wording/grouping, pointer previews, result prose and legacy strength-history read cadence. No exact full-screen/transient parity claim is added.
- Known legacy placement preview `key` errors and accepted combat/Story quirks remain. Authoritative gameplay, privacy exceptions and deterministic entropy rules are unchanged.
- Ring round/statistics labels retain legacy starter-based bookkeeping; starter elimination does not introduce new lap rules.
- Polling and approximate seven-second connection status remain. Disconnected Humans receive no AI takeover or AFK penalty.
- Development utilities remain in their accepted adapters; Auto Match migration is regression-only/deferred. No new dev-tool feature was added.
- Durable recovery is now optional and implemented for controlled single-process use. There is no session expiry/garbage collection, retention UI, encryption-at-rest service, cross-version migration or multi-process recovery. Full snapshots serialize requests and may become slow with many long-running sessions. Restart with a new state directory between playtest campaigns once sessions no longer need retention.
- Physical second-device/router/firewall validation and public hosting remain operator follow-ups. Automated LAN checks use independent browser contexts and the machine's LAN interface.
- Accounts, matchmaking, ratings, Hall of Fame, payments, anti-cheat, DDoS protection and moderation are outside this migration.
- Team Mode/2v2, Chaos Manifestation trigger, progression/upgrades, audio redesign and artwork/UI replacement remain separately authorized future branches.
